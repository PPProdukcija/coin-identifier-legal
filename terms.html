Obverse Terms of Use

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Obverse helps you photograph and learn about coins. Identifications are informed suggestions, not authentication, grading, appraisal, or a guarantee of value. Metal values, where shown, are estimates from published specifications and public spot prices, not collector prices for your particular coin.

Premium subscriptions

Obverse offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable Premium subscriptions for unlimited coin identifications. Available plans, local prices, billing periods, and any eligible trial are shown in the app before you buy. A subscription is charged through your Apple Account and renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. Restore Purchases is available in the app. The first three successful identifications can be used without a subscription.

Use and support

You should only submit photos you have the right to use and should verify important identification details independently. The app depends on a network connection for identification and current metal prices. For support, email support@ppprodukcija.com. See our Privacy Policy for data handling.
