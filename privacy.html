Obverse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Obverse identifies coins from photos you choose. This page explains what happens to those photos and the limited technical data used to run the service.

Coin photos and results

When you tap Identify, the selected front and back photos are combined on your device and sent securely to the Obverse backend. The backend passes the image to OpenAI's API to produce an identification. We set the API request to store: false. Our backend does not save your photos or identification results. OpenAI may process and retain API data under its data controls. Do not submit photos containing personal information you do not want processed for this purpose.

Your collection, history, and their images are saved on your device. You can delete all local records in Settings → Delete All Local Data. Removing the app also removes its local records unless you have a device backup.

Service operation

Obverse creates a random identifier for this app installation and sends it to the backend with requests. The backend also receives your network IP address. Both are used for short-term rate limiting and abuse prevention; they are not sent to OpenAI with your photos. Service logs record a random request ID, outcome and duration, not the photo or installation identifier. The app does not use advertising trackers or send its in-app interaction events to an analytics provider.

For eligible coins, the backend retrieves public gold and silver spot prices from gold-api.com. It does not send coin photos, the installation identifier, or your IP address to that service.

Purchases and support

Subscriptions are handled by Apple's StoreKit. Obverse does not receive your payment card details. If you email support, your email and message are handled only to answer your request. You can request deletion of a support conversation by emailing support@ppprodukcija.com.

Your choices

You choose whether to grant camera access or select photos, and a photo is sent only after you tap Identify. You can stop using identification at any time, revoke camera permission in iOS Settings, and remove your local records in the app. For questions about data handling, contact support@ppprodukcija.com.
