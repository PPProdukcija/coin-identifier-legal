Obverse — privacy

Obverse helps you photograph and learn about coins using evidence-based AI analysis. Photos submitted for identification are processed by the Obverse service and are not stored by the Obverse backend.

Obverse also requests current gold and silver spot prices from gold-api.com to compute a metal value for eligible coins. This is a request for public market prices only — no user data or photos are sent to gold-api.com.

For questions, contact support@ppprodukcija.com.
